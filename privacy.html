Sudoku Flow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Sudoku Flow is designed to provide a simple and enjoyable Sudoku experience while respecting your privacy.

Information Collection

Sudoku Flow does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Game Data

Sudoku Flow may store game-related information locally on your device. This can include your current puzzle, game settings, statistics, trophy progress, preferences, and other information necessary for app features to function.

This information is used to provide features such as continuing a saved puzzle and remembering your preferences.

Tracking

Sudoku Flow does not track you across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Advertising

Sudoku Flow does not contain third-party advertising.

Third-Party Services

Some functionality provided by Apple or the App Store may be subject to Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

Sudoku Flow does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Sudoku Flow evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
