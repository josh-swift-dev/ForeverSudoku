Forever Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Forever Sudoku is designed to provide a simple and enjoyable Sudoku experience. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Game Data

Game progress, saved puzzles, statistics, achievements, trophy progress, and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

This gameplay information is used to provide the features of Forever Sudoku and is not transmitted to the developer.

Accounts

Forever Sudoku does not require you to create an account, register, or sign in with the developer.

Advertising

Forever Sudoku uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process certain information in connection with advertising, such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, diagnostic information, performance information, and interactions with advertisements or the app.

This information may be used by Google for purposes including providing, measuring, improving, and securing advertising services.

Users may have the option to make a one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements from Forever Sudoku.

Tracking and Advertising Preferences

Advertising technologies may use device identifiers or other information as permitted by your device settings, applicable law, and your privacy choices.

Apple provides privacy controls that allow you to manage whether apps may request permission to track activity across other companies' apps and websites.

Third-Party Services

Forever Sudoku uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at: Google Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases

Forever Sudoku offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment card information.

Children's Privacy

Forever Sudoku does not knowingly collect personal information directly from users. Third-party services used by the app may process information as described in their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Forever Sudoku's features, advertising services, or data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
