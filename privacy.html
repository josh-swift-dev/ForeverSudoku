Forever Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Forever Sudoku is designed to provide a simple, private, and distraction-free Sudoku experience.

Information We Collect

Forever Sudoku does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Game Data

Game progress, saved puzzles, statistics, achievements, trophy progress, and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

This information is used only to provide the features of Forever Sudoku and is not transmitted to the developer.

Accounts

Forever Sudoku does not require you to create an account, register, or sign in.

Advertising

Forever Sudoku does not contain third-party advertisements.

Tracking

Forever Sudoku does not track you across apps or websites.

Analytics

Forever Sudoku does not use third-party analytics services to collect personal information or gameplay activity.

Third-Party Services

Forever Sudoku does not use third-party advertising or analytics services that collect personal data.

Children's Privacy

Forever Sudoku does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Forever Sudoku's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
