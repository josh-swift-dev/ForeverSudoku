Forever Sudoku Support

Thanks for playing Forever Sudoku!

Forever Sudoku is designed to provide a clean, simple Sudoku experience with unlimited boards, multiple difficulty levels, gameplay customization, and achievements to unlock.

Starting a Puzzle

From the main screen, tap New Board and choose your difficulty:

  • Easy
  • Medium
  • Hard
  • Expert

A new Sudoku puzzle will be generated directly on your device.

How to Play

Select an empty square and tap a number to enter it.

The goal is to fill the board so that every row, every column, and every 3×3 box contains the numbers 1 through 9 without duplicates.

Notes

Tap Notes to enable Notes mode.

While Notes mode is enabled, tapping a number adds it as a candidate for the selected square. Tap the same number again to remove it.

Notes are pencil marks and do not count as mistakes.

Undo

Use the Undo button to reverse your previous move.

Hints

Forever Sudoku includes optional hints. A hint can reveal the correct value for an editable square.

Hint availability and the number of hints available per board can be adjusted in Settings.

Mistakes

Mistake tracking can be enabled or disabled in Settings.

When enabled, you can also choose the number of mistakes allowed before a board ends.

Timer

Forever Sudoku includes an optional game timer so you can track how long each puzzle takes to complete.

The timer can be shown or hidden in Settings.

Saved Games

Your current puzzle is stored locally on your device when you leave the game or return to the main screen.

Tap Continue Previous from the main screen to return to your saved puzzle.

Trophies and Achievements

Forever Sudoku includes 16 trophies that can be unlocked by completing different challenges.

Some achievements are hidden until you discover them. Your Trophy Room tracks your progress, victories, streaks, and unlocked achievements.

Unlock every trophy to earn the Crown.

Customization

Open Settings to customize your Forever Sudoku experience. Available options include:

  • Color themes
  • Dark Mode
  • Timer
  • Hints
  • Mistake tracking
  • Related-cell highlighting
  • Matching-number highlighting

Privacy

Forever Sudoku does not require an account and does not collect or sell your personal information.

Your puzzle progress and app settings are stored locally on your device.
