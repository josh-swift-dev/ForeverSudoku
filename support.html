Sudoku Flow Support

Need help with Sudoku Flow? Here are answers to some common questions and a quick guide to using the app.

How to Play

Fill the board so every row, column, and 3×3 box contains the numbers 1 through 9 without repeating a number.

Tap an empty square and then select a number from the number pad.

Starting a New Game

From the Sudoku Flow home screen, select New Board and choose your desired difficulty.

Available difficulty levels include Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert.

Notes

Notes allow you to enter possible numbers into a square without committing to an answer.

Turn Notes on and tap numbers to add them as pencil marks. Tap a penciled number again to remove it.

Hints

Hints can reveal a correct value when you need some help. The number of hints available per board can be customized in Settings.

Mistakes

Sudoku Flow can track incorrect entries while you play. Mistake tracking and the allowed mistake limit can be customized from Settings.

Undo

Use Undo to reverse your most recent move.

Trophies

Complete challenges while playing to unlock trophies. Visit the Trophy Room from the main screen to view your achievements and progress.

Dark Mode & Themes

Appearance options, including Dark Mode and available themes, can be changed from Settings.

Saved Games

Sudoku Flow automatically saves your current puzzle locally. Use Continue Previous from the home screen to return to an unfinished game.

Still Need Help?

If you encounter a problem, please include a short description of what happened and, if possible, your iPhone model and iOS version.
